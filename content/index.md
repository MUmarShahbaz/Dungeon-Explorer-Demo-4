>[!info]
>This project is a video game that was made with scalability in mind. The goal of the project  was never the game, rather it was to create powerful and reusable components which will make further additions extremely easy and standardized. The main target in this project was the [[ECS]] (Entity Component System) which allows me to program a custom character in less than 10 minutes!! Eventually, after some further testing and development, I will release the best components as a library for Godot and maybe even create a course on Game Development!!
>So please do not vote without reading the [[ECS]] page!! (but don't forget to enjoy the game!!)

>[!warning]
>To my fellow HackClubbers, I have read your previous votes and have been actively working to fix them! However there are certain things that you believe to be bugs, but actually they're due to limitations and I hope that you won't want reduce my score based on that. Such as overlapping of mobs and OP shield was made so that you can finish the game fast; The dialogues always show a knight because I don't have the art for others prepared just yet!!. The audio is not complete as you will notice only swords and arrows make sounds. Other than these, you can always quit game and then skip ahead to another mission if you get stuck somewhere!

# Brief

The Elf King is a powerful but kind man who has held the kingdom together for a thousand years. Suddenly the King catches an illness so potent that even the Royal Physicians have been left dumbfounded. The Elf Queen calls upon the Kingdoms strongest fighter to find the Dark Lady in the deepest chamber of the Ancient Dungeon. YOU! YES, YOU! You must make your way through hordes of monsters to reach the depth and meet the only one who can save your King. However, something doesn't feel right...........
